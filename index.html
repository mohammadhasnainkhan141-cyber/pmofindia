Before the republic

The story of
India’s first PM

Jawaharlal Nehru 1889—1964

On the night India became independent, Jawaharlal Nehru became its first prime minister.

Born in Allahabad (now Prayagraj) on 14 November 1889, Nehru studied in England before returning to India. He joined the Indian National Congress and the independence movement, working alongside Mahatma Gandhi and spending years in prison under British rule.

As Congress president at the 1929 Lahore session, Nehru became closely associated with the call for complete independence. On 15 August 1947, he took office as India’s first prime minister. He led the new country through its early years, supporting parliamentary institutions, economic planning, scientific development and a foreign policy of non-alignment.

Nehru remained prime minister until his death on 27 May 1964. His long tenure helped shape independent India, and his legacy continues to be discussed and debated.

01 — The background

From Vadnagar
to New Delhi

Narendra Damodardas Modi was born in Vadnagar, in the present-day state of Gujarat, on 17 September 1950.

As a young man, he became involved with the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS). He later joined the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), where he worked in the party organisation. In 2001, he became Chief Minister of Gujarat, a position he held until 2014.

In the 2014 general election, the BJP won a majority in the Lok Sabha and Modi became prime minister on 26 May 2014. He was sworn in for a second term in May 2019. Following the 2024 general election, he took oath for a third consecutive term on 9 June 2024, leading a government formed by the National Democratic Alliance (NDA).

03 — Policies & public life

Initiatives and
major events

Governments led by Modi have introduced a range of programmes and made significant policy decisions. Here are some widely known examples.

012014

Jan Dhan Yojana

A financial inclusion programme intended to expand access to basic bank accounts and related services.

022014

Swachh Bharat Mission

A national sanitation and cleanliness campaign, including efforts to expand household toilet access.

032015

Digital India

A programme to develop digital infrastructure, make public services available online, and promote digital literacy.

042016

Ujjwala Yojana

A scheme that provides connections for liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) to eligible households.

052017

Goods and Services Tax

A nationwide indirect-tax framework introduced in 2017, replacing multiple central and state taxes.

062018

Ayushman Bharat

A health programme that includes health and wellness centres and publicly funded hospital coverage for eligible families.

072019

PM-KISAN

An income-support scheme providing eligible farmer families with direct benefit transfers.

082021

PM Gati Shakti

A national master plan intended to coordinate infrastructure planning and improve multimodal connectivity.

Also part of the public record

Major events during his premiership include the 2016 demonetisation of specified banknotes; the 2019 change to Jammu and Kashmir’s constitutional status and reorganisation; the COVID-19 pandemic; the repeal of three farm laws in 2021 after sustained protests; and India’s G20 presidency and summit in 2023. These events have been subject to debate and differing assessments.
